Privacy Policy for Book It

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Book It respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have when using the Book It mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Book It, we may collect the following information:

  • Name or profile information provided by you
  • Phone number
  • Booking and reservation information
  • Information about selected computer clubs, booking time, duration, and selected computers
  • Account activity, including completed, cancelled, or missed reservations
  • Device and technical information necessary for the operation, security, and improvement of the application
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting customer support

Book It may also process payment-related information when you choose to pay for services through a supported payment provider. Book It does not necessarily store your full bank card details. Payment information may be processed directly by third-party payment providers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your Book It account
  • Process and manage reservations
  • Show available computer clubs and available computers
  • Confirm, modify, or cancel bookings
  • Provide relevant reservation information to participating computer clubs
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, spam, and misuse of the platform
  • Improve the functionality and reliability of Book It
  • Provide customer support
  • Send important service-related notifications
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

3. Sharing of Information

We may share limited information with computer clubs when necessary to process and manage your reservation.

For example, a participating club may receive information such as:

  • Your booking details
  • Booking time and duration
  • Selected computer or seat
  • Information necessary to identify or confirm your reservation

We may also share information with service providers that help us operate Book It, including hosting providers, analytics services, notification services, and payment providers.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Payments

Payments made through Book It may be processed by third-party payment providers.

These providers may collect payment information according to their own privacy policies and security standards.

Book It may receive information confirming whether a payment was successful, but we do not intend to store full payment card details unless specifically required for the operation of the service and permitted by applicable law.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no electronic system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the service, fulfill legal obligations, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse.

6. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Book It account and associated personal information.

To request deletion, contact us using the email address provided below.

We may retain certain information when required by law, for fraud prevention, financial recordkeeping, dispute resolution, or legitimate security purposes.

7. Location Information

Book It may use location-related information to help users find computer clubs, display nearby clubs, or provide map functionality.

Depending on your device settings, location access may require your explicit permission.

You may disable location permissions through your device settings, although some location-based features may no longer function correctly.

8. Notifications

Book It may send notifications related to:

  • Booking confirmations
  • Upcoming reservations
  • Booking changes
  • Cancellations
  • Important service information

Users may manage notification permissions through their device settings.

9. Third-Party Services

Book It may use third-party services, including:

  • Payment providers
  • Mapping services
  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Analytics services
  • Push notification services

These third-party services may process limited information according to their own privacy policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Book It is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If we learn that personal information has been collected from a child without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised.

Continued use of Book It after changes are published may constitute acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy where permitted by applicable law.
