Delete Your Book It Account & Data

This page is for users of the Book It app (uz.bookit.app) who want their account and the personal data associated with it deleted.

How to request deletion

Send an email from any address, telling us the phone number or username your Book It account is registered with:

Request deletion by email

Email: zokirovdodash@gmail.com — subject "Delete my Book It account". We may contact you to confirm you own the account before deleting it.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (name, username, phone number, avatar)
  • Your reservation history linked to you personally
  • Your uploaded photos

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Records we must keep by law (for example financial records of paid bookings), for the legally required period
  • Minimal records needed for fraud prevention or resolving disputes
  • Anonymous statistics that no longer identify you

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

Удаляются: аккаунт, история бронирований и загруженные фото. Данные, которые мы обязаны хранить по закону (например, финансовые записи), сохраняются на установленный законом срок. Запросы обрабатываются в течение 30 дней.
